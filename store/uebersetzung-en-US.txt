When can you finally clock out?
Clock-Out Calculator answers exactly that question. In two seconds, beautifully and without clutter.

HOW IT WORKS
• Tap your start time (e.g. 06:44)
• Work time and break are already set (default: 8 h + 45 min)
• Instantly see your clock-out time – with a live countdown ring

MAKE IT YOURS
• Freely adjustable work time and break
• Quick pick “6 h, no break”
• Night shift? No problem – the app calculates across midnight
• Daily target for your overtime account
• Home screen widget: your clock-out time always in view

CLOCK-OUT PRO – ONE-TIME, NO SUBSCRIPTION
• Unlimited profiles, e.g. Mon–Thu, Friday, Night shift
• Overtime account with weekly overview and one-tap “Book today”
• Automatic legal break calculation based on the German Working Hours Act (over 6 h → 30 min, over 9 h → 45 min)
• Your own reminders: e.g. 1 h before, at halftime and right at clock-out – with a fun quote
• Live countdown right in the home screen widget
• No ads
• Try it free for 24 hours first – with a short video

PURE GOOD MOOD
• Fun quotes for your job: office, trades, IT, care & health, teaching, hospitality, retail, shift work and more
• Modern design, pleasant colors and a beautiful dark mode

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• No account, no sign-up

• Works offline
